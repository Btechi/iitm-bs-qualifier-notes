## Week 1: Vowel sounds

*asked in every paper in past qualifier papers*

*Diagram: Where each vowel sits.* [View it on BTechi](https://www.btechi.in/qualifier/notes/english-1#vowels).

**Short vowels**

| Symbol | IPA | As in | Words from past papers |
|---|---|---|---|
| i | /ɪ/ | sit | pit, kit, knit, lit, mitt |
| e | /e/ | bed | wet, pen |
| a | /æ/ | cat | pack, man |
| o | /ɒ/ | hot | bottle, pot |
| u | /ʊ/ | put | **foot**, book, good |
| ʌ | /ʌ/ | cup | but, love, son |
| ə (schwa) | /ə/ | about | sofa, teacher (unstressed syllables) |

**Long vowels: the letter is doubled**

| Symbol | IPA | As in | Words from past papers |
|---|---|---|---|
| ii | /iː/ | seat | meet, lead, wheat, neat, feet, bleat |
| aa | /ɑː/ | car | **hard**, father, start |
| oo | /ɔː/ | law | **court**, board, hoard, sport, ball |
| uu | /uː/ | food | root, loot, moot, blue, too, flew, through |
| əə | /ɜː/ | bird | her, word, learn |

**Example: PYQ answers (2023–24)**

Word with a 'u' sound: Root / Foot / Court? · With an 'oo' sound: Court / Loot / Cat / Feat? · With 'aa': Hoard / Board / Hard / Teeth? · Without 'ii': Meet / Lead / Wet / Wheat? · Same vowel as 'pack': Cat / Bleat / Shear / Boat?

1. Foot has short /ʊ/; root has long uu.
2. Court has /ɔː/, which the course writes as oo. Loot is uu.
3. Hard has /ɑː/ (aa).
4. Wet has short e; the others have ii.
5. Pack and cat share /æ/.

**Answer:** Foot · Court · Hard · Wet · Cat

> [!WARNING]
> **Common traps**
> - Spelling lies: 'oo' in foot is short u /ʊ/, but in food and root it's long uu /uː/.
> - The course's **oo** is the vowel in law, court and board, **not** the one in food.
> - Odd one out by vowel: knit, pit, kit vs neat; lit, mitt, knit vs feet. The long ii word is the odd one.
> - All of through, blue, too and flew carry uu (PYQ May 2023: 'All of these').

## Week 1: Diphthongs

*asked in every paper in past qualifier papers*

- A **diphthong** glides from one vowel to another inside one syllable (b-oy, m-ou-th). A pure vowel holds still (sp-or-t, m-oo-t).

*Diagram: The 8 diphthongs as glides.* Each arrow is the tongue moving from the first vowel to the second, inside one syllable. [View it on BTechi](https://www.btechi.in/qualifier/notes/english-1#diphthongs).

**The 8 diphthongs**

| Symbol | IPA | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| ei | /eɪ/ | day, bail, maiden, wake, cake |
| ai | /aɪ/ | my, bite, fly, tired |
| oi | /ɔɪ/ | boy, boycott, loiter, coin |
| ou | /əʊ/ | go, boat, coach, both, know |
| au | /aʊ/ | now, mouth, south, shout, snout, stout, pout |
| iə | /ɪə/ | near, dear, here, gear, shear |
| eə | /eə/ | hair, pear, care, bear |
| uə | /ʊə/ | tour, cure, pure |

**Example: PYQ answers: 'Which word has no diphthong?'**

Snout / Stout / Sport / Shout (asked Sep 2023 and Jan 2024) · Mouth / South / Pout / Moot (May 2024) · Loiter / Stew / Maiden (May 2023) · Which have a diphthong: Bottle / Boycott / Bail (May 2023)?

1. Sport has the pure vowel /ɔː/.
2. Moot has the pure vowel /uː/.
3. Stew is /stjuː/: a glide consonant plus pure uu.
4. Boycott (oi) and bail (ei) glide; bottle has short o.

**Answer:** Sport · Moot · Stew · Both Boycott and Bail

> [!TIP]
> Say the vowel slowly. If your jaw or tongue moves during it, it's a diphthong. If it stays put, it isn't.

## Week 1: Consonants, clusters & glides

*asked in every paper in past qualifier papers*

*Diagram: Places of articulation, lips to throat.* [View it on BTechi](https://www.btechi.in/qualifier/notes/english-1#consonants).

**Place of articulation (this decides 'odd one out by initial sound')**

| Place | Sounds | Example words |
|---|---|---|
| Bilabial (both lips) | p, b, m, w | peat, beat, pear, man, wet |
| Labiodental (lip + teeth) | f, v | fan, van, phone |
| Dental (tongue + teeth) | θ, ð | thin, this |
| Alveolar (tongue + ridge behind teeth) | t, d, n, s, z, l, r | town, down, noun, near, dear, tear, sun |
| Post-alveolar | ʃ, ʒ, tʃ, dʒ | she, measure, church, judge |
| Palatal | j (written /y/) | yes, you |
| Velar (back of tongue + soft palate) | k, g, ŋ | gown, gear, clear, king, sing |
| Glottal | h | hat |

**Example: PYQ answers: odd one out by initial sound**

near, dear, tear, gear (May 2024) · noun, down, town, gown (Jan 2024) · peat, beat, clear, pear (May 2024)

1. n, d, t are alveolar; g is velar.
2. n, d, t are alveolar; g is velar.
3. p, b are bilabial; clear starts with velar /k/ (and a cluster).

**Answer:** Gear · Gown · Clear

**Manner of articulation**

| Manner | Sounds |
|---|---|
| Plosive (stop, then burst) | p b t d k g |
| Nasal (air through the nose) | m n ŋ |
| Fricative (friction) | f v θ ð s z ʃ ʒ h |
| Affricate (stop + friction) | tʃ dʒ |
| Approximant | l r w j |
| Voiceless / voiced pairs | p/b, t/d, k/g, f/v, s/z, θ/ð, ʃ/ʒ, tʃ/dʒ |

*Diagram: Linking glides.* [View it on BTechi](https://www.btechi.in/qualifier/notes/english-1#consonants).

**Linking glides between words**

| | Formula | Note |
|---|---|---|
| /w/ glide | after u, uu, ou, au (rounded lips) + a vowel | go‿out → go-w-out (PYQ Sep 2023) · do‿it · who‿is |
| /y/ glide | after i, ii, ei, ai, oi (spread lips) + a vowel | she‿offered → she-y-offered (PYQ Jan 2024) · me‿off (PYQ May 2024) · I‿am · they‿are |

- **Consonant cluster:** two or more consonants together in one syllable with no vowel between: **cl**ear, **str**eet, **spr**ing, te**xt** (PYQ: TRUE).
- Compare first **sounds**, not letters: knit starts with /n/, city with /s/, chemistry with /k/, phone with /f/.
- Silent letters: **k**nit, **w**rite, thum**b**, **h**our, lis**t**en, **p**sychology.
- Phone numbers: any clear grouping works (86/37/68/11/34, 863/768/113/4, 8637/6811/34). Say 'double 1' for 11. PYQ answer: 'All of these'.

## Week 2: Nouns & pronouns

*asked in every paper in past qualifier papers*

*Diagram: Types of nouns.* [View it on BTechi](https://www.btechi.in/qualifier/notes/english-1#nouns-pronouns).

**Types of nouns**

| Type | What it names | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Proper | A specific name (capital letter) | Shraddha, Raghav, Chennai |
| Common | A general kind of thing | student, city, book, walk |
| Collective | A group as one unit | team, jury, herd, committee, **parliament** |
| Abstract | An idea, quality or state | goodness, youth, poverty, success, happiness |
| Material | A substance | gold, wood, water |
| Compound | Two or more words acting as one noun | **swimming pool**, toothpaste, mother-in-law |
| Uncountable | Can't be counted one by one | advice, information, furniture, luggage |

**Pronouns**

| Kind | Words |
|---|---|
| Personal | I, you, he, she, it, we, they · me, him, her, us, them |
| Possessive (stand alone) | mine, **yours**, his, hers, ours, theirs: 'That is yours.' |
| Possessive (before a noun) | my, your, his, her, our, their: the answer keys mark these **Pronoun** |
| Reflexive | myself, yourself, themselves |
| Demonstrative | this, that, these, those |
| Interrogative | **who** (subject), whom (object), whose, which, what |
| Relative | who, whom, whose, which, that |
| Indefinite | someone, anyone, everyone, nobody, each |

**Example: PYQ answers**

Not an abstract noun: Goodness / Youth / Poverty / Parliament? · Noun in 'Working hard can bring you success'? · Nouns in 'Shraddha is a brilliant student'? · 'This is my letter and that is ___' · '___ wrote The Canterbury Tales?' · 'Their fans were very disappointed': Their is a…?

1. Parliament is a collective noun.
2. Success (option list: Working, Hard, Bring, Success).
3. Two answers: Shraddha **and** student (multi-select).
4. Yours: a stand-alone possessive.
5. Who: asks about the subject.
6. Pronoun.

**Answer:** Parliament · Success · Shraddha + Student · Yours · Who · Pronoun

> [!WARNING]
> **Common traps**
> - 'Swimming pool' is a compound noun, not adjective + noun.
> - yours, hers, theirs never take an apostrophe.
> - Multi-select: when a sentence has two nouns, tick both.

## Week 2: Verbs, tenses & modals

*asked in every paper in past qualifier papers*

**Modals**

| Modal | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| can | ability; informal permission | Sudha **can** speak Telugu. (PYQ) |
| could | past ability; polite request | Could you help me? |
| may | formal permission; possibility | **May** I ask a question? (PYQ) |
| might | weak possibility | It might rain. |
| must | obligation; strong certainty | You must wear a helmet. |
| should | advice, duty | You should rest. |
| will / shall | future; offers with I/we | Shall we go? |
| would | polite request; hypothetical | Would you like tea? |
| ought to | moral duty | We ought to help. |

*Diagram: Tense signals on a timeline.* [View it on BTechi](https://www.btechi.in/qualifier/notes/english-1#verbs).

**Tense signals**

| Signal words | Tense | Example |
|---|---|---|
| since, for, ever, never, just, already, yet | Present perfect | She has not read anything **since** Monday. (PYQ) |
| yesterday, last year, ago, in 2010 | Past simple | They **bought** the house last year. (PYQ) |
| now, at the moment | Present continuous | He is writing now. |
| every day, usually, always | Present simple | She usually walks to work. |
| by + a future time | Future perfect | By next June, she will have completed her degree. |

**Agreement and form rules**

| | Formula | Note |
|---|---|---|
| A as well as / along with / with B | the verb agrees with **A** | 'The teacher, as well as the students, **is** responsible.' (PYQ) |
| Either … or / neither … nor | the verb agrees with the **nearer** subject |  |
| Each, every, everyone, one of the | singular verb |  |
| The number of / a number of | singular / plural |  |
| get / have + object + past participle | causative | 'We will get it **done**.' (PYQ) |

**Confusables**

| Pair | Rule |
|---|---|
| tell / say | tell + a person ('Don't **tell** me…', PYQ); say + the words ('say that…') |
| affect / effect | affect = verb, to influence ('The storm **affected** the coast', PYQ); effect = noun, a result |
| lie / lay | lie = rest (no object); lay = put something down |
| rise / raise | rise has no object; raise needs one |

> [!WARNING]
> **Common traps**
> - 'Since' needs a starting point (since Monday); 'for' needs a length of time (for two days).
> - Never use the past simple with since/for/ever: 'I have seen it', not 'I saw it ever'.

## Week 2: Adjectives & adverbs

*asked in every paper in past qualifier papers*

- An **adjective** describes a noun: **ripe** tomatoes; the house was painted **yellow** (PYQs).
- An **adverb** modifies a verb, an adjective or another adverb: walk **carefully**, walked **slowly**, **very** happy.
- Kinds of adverb: manner (slowly), place (here), time (yesterday), frequency (usually), degree (very, quite, too).
- 'Rinku reads **quite clearly**': both quite and clearly are adverbs (PYQ, multi-select).
- 'Joseph ran **faster** than everyone': faster is a comparative adverb, because it modifies ran (PYQ).
- 'She **yelled** when she hit her toe': yelled is a verb (PYQ).

*Diagram: What each one modifies.* [View it on BTechi](https://www.btechi.in/qualifier/notes/english-1#adjectives-adverbs).

**Degrees of comparison**

| Positive | Comparative | Superlative |
|---|---|---|
| good / well | better | best |
| bad | worse | worst |
| many / much | more | most |
| little | less | least |
| far | farther / further | farthest / furthest |
| fast | **faster** (PYQ) | fastest |

**Making -ly adverbs**

| Adjective | Adverb |
|---|---|
| suitable | **suitably** (PYQ) |
| happy | happily |
| true | truly |
| gentle | gently |
| basic | basically |

> [!WARNING]
> **Common traps**
> - Comparative + than: 'better than', never 'more better'.
> - Superlatives take 'the': 'the best chocolate cake' (PYQ).
> - too = excessively or also ('**too** many people', PYQ); to = towards or infinitive; two = 2.
> - Not every -ly word is an adverb: friendly, lovely and lonely are adjectives.

## Week 3: Articles: a, an, the

*asked in every paper in past qualifier papers*

| | Formula | Note |
|---|---|---|
| a | before a consonant **sound** | a laptop, a cold day, a book, a university, a European, a one-way street |
| an | before a vowel **sound** | an emergency exit, an hour, an honest man, an MBA, an umbrella |
| the: specific | something already known or identified | 'Pass me the book on the table.' |
| the: unique | only one exists | the Sun, the Earth, the sky, the east |
| the: rank | superlatives and ordinals | the best, the first, the only |
| the: geography | rivers, seas, oceans, mountain ranges, island groups | the Ganga, the Himalayas, holidays in the mountains |
| No article | single mountains, most countries and cities, languages, meals, sports, general abstract ideas | Mount Everest, India, English, breakfast, cricket |

*Diagram: Picking the article.* a/an go by the first sound, not the first letter. [View it on BTechi](https://www.btechi.in/qualifier/notes/english-1#articles).

**Example: PYQ answers**

There is __ emergency exit · This is __ best chocolate cake · __ Sun rises in the east · Tomorrow will be __ cold day · I want to buy __ laptop · They spend their holidays in __ mountains · He gave me __ book on my birthday

1. Vowel sound → an.
2. Superlative → the.
3. Unique → the.
4. Consonant sound, one of many days → a.
5. Any laptop → a.
6. Mountain range → the.
7. Any book → a.

**Answer:** an · the · The · a · a · the · a

## Week 3: Prepositions & conjunctions

*asked in every paper in past qualifier papers*

*Diagram: at, on, in: from wide to exact.* [View it on BTechi](https://www.btechi.in/qualifier/notes/english-1#prepositions-conjunctions).

**Prepositions of time and place**

| Use | When | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| at | an exact time or point | at 5 pm, at night, at the station |
| on | days, dates, surfaces | on Monday, on 5 May, on the table |
| in | months, years, seasons, parts of the day, inside | in May, in 2024, in the morning, **in** the fridge (PYQ) |
| since / for | starting point / length of time | since Monday / for two hours |
| ahead of | before someone in a line | standing in line **ahead** of me (PYQ) |

**Fixed phrases**

| Phrase | Example |
|---|---|
| worried **about** | Sushma is worried about the exam. (PYQ) |
| **in** my opinion | In my opinion, she is clever. (PYQ) |
| look **into** | Look into the matter (investigate). (PYQ) |
| interested in · good at · afraid of | She is good at maths but afraid of heights. |
| depend on · responsible for · different from | Who is responsible for this? |
| angry **with** a person · **about** a thing | She was angry with him about the delay. |

**Conjunctions**

| Kind | Words | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Coordinating (FANBOYS) | for, and, nor, but, or, yet, so | He may be poor **but** his character is faultless. (PYQ) |
| Correlative (pairs) | both…and, either…or, neither…nor, not only…but also | The culprit was **both** fined **and** punished. (PYQ) |
| Subordinating | because, although, if, unless, while, until, as if, so that | She behaves **as if** she was the captain. (PYQ) |

> [!WARNING]
> **Common traps**
> - 'As if' introduces an imagined comparison; plain 'as' means 'in the way that' or 'because'.
> - 'Beyond' in 'beyond Zuckerberg's post' is a preposition (PYQ May 2024).

<a id="reading"></a>

## Week 4: Reading comprehension & conversations

*asked in every paper in past qualifier papers*

**Strategy**

- Read the questions first, then the passage, so you know what to look for.
- Answers are paraphrased: match the meaning, not the exact words.
- 'NOT TRUE' or 'EXCEPT': find each option in the passage; the one you can't find (or that contradicts it) is the answer.
- True/False: a single wrong detail (a name, number or place) makes the whole statement false.
- Use only the passage, never outside knowledge.

**Conversation fill-ins: phrases that fit**

| Situation | Phrase |
|---|---|
| Answering a formal call | Good morning, XYZ Hospital. How may I help you? |
| Asking for someone | May I speak to Dr. …, please? |
| Asking the caller to wait | Could you please hold the line? / Please hold on a moment. |
| Taking a message | Would you like to leave a message? / May I know who's calling? |
| Booking | I'd like to book an appointment for… |
| Didn't catch it | Could you repeat that, please? |
| Closing | Thank you for calling. Have a nice day. |
| Informal reply | I'm doing great, thanks. How about you? |

> [!TIP]
> For each blank, read the line **after** it. The reply tells you what the blank said: 'Sure, I'll hold' means the blank asked them to hold.

> [!WARNING]
> **Common traps**
> - Match the register: hospital, office or professor ⇒ formal ('May I…', 'Could you please…'); siblings or friends ⇒ informal ('Hey', 'Cool').

## Last-minute checklist

- [ ] Long vowels are doubled: ii (seat), aa (car), oo (law, court), uu (food), əə (bird).
- [ ] 'u' is the vowel in foot; 'uu' is the vowel in food.
- [ ] No-diphthong answers so far: sport, moot, stew, bottle.
- [ ] /w/ glide after rounded vowels (go‿out); /y/ glide after i-type vowels (she‿offered, me‿off).
- [ ] Odd one out by initial sound = different place of articulation (gear, gown, clear are velar).
- [ ] a/an go by sound: an hour, an MBA, a university.
- [ ] 'The' for unique things, superlatives, rivers and mountain ranges.
- [ ] 'A as well as B' → the verb agrees with A.
- [ ] Since + starting point; for + length of time.
- [ ] Separable test: put 'it' in the middle (sum it up ✓).
- [ ] Collocations: weather forecast, unanimous decision, bank account, strong coffee, short temper.
- [ ] RC 'NOT TRUE' questions: eliminate what you can find in the passage.
